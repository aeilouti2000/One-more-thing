Try it

  • Drag the screen left or right, or tap the switch: the two pages slide, the pill and the header follow.
  • Tap ‹ and › beside the month: the ring, the total and the category bar animate to the new month.
  • Go back to July: a month older than the plan keeps shows its saved summary only.
  • Tap a category chip to filter the purchases.
  • Scroll the purchases to the end: the next page loads from the server.
  • Tap the search icon, then type.
  • On Invoices, tap a receipt card to open the trip, then go back.
